The real Rheia firmware (sound, sequencer and screens) compiled for the browser. Sound starts when you power on.

Playing it

KeysA…; and Z…N white keys, Q…[ black keys
Buttons1…6 FX SEL ENV LFO EDIT GLO · 7 HOME 8 SAVE 9 ARP 0 SEQ · Space PLAY Enter REC · - = OCT− OCT+
Knobsdrag up / down, the mouse wheel, or the arrow keys when focused
Holda button held on the computer keyboard stays held; with a mouse, Shift-click holds it until clicked again

What is different from the FM-1

Nothing is saved: projects, presets and settings last until the page is closed. No MIDI, USB audio or firmware updates. The drum track's GEN page (BEAT GEN) is on the DRUMS screen: select track 4 with ALGORITHM, tap SEQ until gen is lit.
