RHEIA

Groovebox firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1, tuned for blackened hardcore, industrial, sludge and noise: blast beats and D-beats from the drum track's BEAT GEN page, on top of everything SLOOP does. Version .

EmulatorTry the firmware in the browser: screens, sequencer and sound, no FM-1 needed. InstallerPut Rheia on your FM-1 over USB (Chrome or Edge). Back up first. EditorSounds, patterns, samples and backups from the computer.

A beta: install at your own risk. Free software (GPL-3.0) — source. Based on SLOOP and Felucca. M-VAVE and FM-1 are trademarks of their owners; Rheia is not affiliated with them.
